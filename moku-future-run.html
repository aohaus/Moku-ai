HOW TO PLAY

Master the 3-lane rush with Moku's cute pitter-patter feet!

👆

SWIPE UP / [▲] / Space

Hop & leap over low barriers, paint buckets, and hurdles with a cute squash bounce.

👇

SWIPE DOWN / [▼] / Down Arrow

Duck & slide under high laser gates, neon beams, and low overhead arches.

👈👉

SWIPE LEFT & RIGHT / Arrow Keys

Switch between the 3 lanes quickly to dodge tall roadblocks and grab items.

🚄

TRAIN ROOF RUNNING

Use ramps or mochi springboards to run on top of trains for bonus points & berries!

❌ DANGEROUS OBSTACLES (AVOID!):
• Heavy BLACK metal barricades with YELLOW & BLACK HAZARD STRIPES and red sirens.
• Jump over low hurdles, slide under high overhead beams, or switch lanes to survive.

✅ HELPFUL ITEMS (TOUCH & COLLECT!):
• 🫐 Glowing Berries: Safe points, score multipliers, and wardrobe currency!
• 🚀 Boost Rocket: Invincible smash speed + mega points!
• 🍡 Mochi: Absorbs 1 hit + launches springboard super-jumps!
• ▲ Glowing Ramps: Safe boarding paths onto train roofs!
