HOW TO PLAY

Master the 3-lane rush with Moku's cute pitter-patter feet!

👆

SWIPE UP / [▲] / Space

Hop over LOW hurdles (▲ on top).

👇

SWIPE DOWN / [▼] / Down Arrow

Slide under HIGH hurdles (▼ underneath).

👈👉

SWIPE LEFT & RIGHT / Arrow Keys

Switch between the 3 lanes to dodge hurdles and grab berries & items.

✨

NARROW MISS!

Jump or slide at the very last moment for bonus points. Keep the combo going to multiply your score (up to x3)!

⚡

SPEED UP & NEW PLACES

Moku runs a little faster every 50m. Every 500m the world changes: sunset grove, starlit forest...

❌ DANGEROUS OBSTACLES (AVOID!):
• HURDLES with YELLOW & BLACK STRIPES and red lamps.
• LOW hurdle = JUMP over it. HIGH hurdle = SLIDE under it.

✅ HELPFUL ITEMS (TOUCH & COLLECT!):
• 🫐 Glowing Berries: Safe points and a bigger score!
• 🚀 Boost Rocket: Invincible smash speed + mega points!
• 🍡 Mochi: Absorbs 1 hit + one super-high bounce!
