How to Play

  • 🪟Each step has two glass panels. Only one is strong!
  • 🕹️Push the stick left or right, then let go to hop.
  • ☁️Pick the weak one and Moku falls into the soft clouds. A fall costs 3 Moints.
  • 🧠Moku remembers every panel he tried. Try again and get farther!
  • 🏁Reach the goal to win 100+ Moints. Lucky guesses give bonus Moints.
  • 💡Hint: the strong panel glows for a moment.
  • 🎁Having a tough time? Miss a lot on one bridge and Hint and Magic Carpet go 50% OFF (one of each).
  • ✨Magic Carpet: flies Moku 3 glasses ahead. The glasses you skip stay a secret!
  • 🍀Luckiest Steps: lucky guesses in a row. Beat your record and show it off in your profile!
