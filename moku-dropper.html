How to Play

🕹️Move Moku with the joystick (or WASD / arrow keys).
🕳️Walk into the hole and drop down the Great Tree.
🪵Fall through the gaps. If you touch a floor or a snack, bonk! Back to the top.
🔮Land at the bottom and step into the purple portal.
🏁In a race, be the first into the portal!
👆Drag the screen to look around. Pinch (or scroll) to zoom. Double-tap to reset.
⭐Stars: reach the portal · no bonks · win the race (or beat the goal time).
