❓ How to Play
↻ Get the latest version
Moku doesn't just govern this town — Moku lives here. Each level gives you one goal: build up the town, but keep enough forest and parks standing to keep Nature healthy. Let Nature drop too far and both the economy and Moku's mood suffer. Leveling up unlocks new buildings — School, Landmark, Restaurant, Moku Gift Shop, Highway, Station, Hotel, Airport — and eventually a couple of distant islands once the mainland fills up. Watch out for the occasional fire, flood, storm, or earthquake, which can damage buildings and cost coins.

Tap a tile to build. Drag to look around, pinch to zoom, and tap ↶ Undo if you tap the wrong tile.
