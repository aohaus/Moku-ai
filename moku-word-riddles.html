How to Play

CYCLE CYCLE=BICYCLE
Two (bi) cycles!
  1. Look at the words and pictures. Where are they? How big? How many?
  2. Type the hidden word or phrase and press Check.
  3. Stuck? Use a 💡 Hint, then 🔤 Choices. They cost Moints.
  4. Too hard? ⏭ Skip shows the answer. You can try it again later.
  5. Every correct answer gives you points and Moints!
  6. 👥 Riddle Race: play with friends at the same time. The first right answer wins each riddle!
